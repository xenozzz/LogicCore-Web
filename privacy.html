Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 February 2026

1. Introduction

LogicCore is committed to protecting your personal data. This page explains what we collect, why we collect it, and your rights.

2. What we collect

We don’t use ad trackers or third-party analytics cookies. The personal data we collect is what you submit through the contact form:

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Phone number (optional)
  • Message details related to your enquiry

3. How we use your information

Contact submissions are processed by Web3Forms and sent to our secure inbox so we can reply, provide estimates, and discuss your project needs.

We do not sell or rent your personal data.

4. Hosting and infrastructure

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like most hosts, technical server logs may include IP addresses for security and service reliability.

5. Your rights

Under UK GDPR, you can request access to your data and, where applicable, request deletion. Contact us at hello@logiccore.cloud.
