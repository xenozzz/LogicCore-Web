Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 February 2026

1. Introduction

LogicCore ("we", "us", or "our") respects your privacy. This policy explains what personal data we process when you use our website and contact us.

2. Data we collect

We run a static website and do not use tracking cookies or advertising pixels. The personal data we receive is data you submit voluntarily through our contact form, which may include:

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Business details you provide
  • Your message or enquiry content

3. How we use your data

We use submitted data only to respond to enquiries, provide quotes, and discuss delivery of relevant services. We do not sell or rent your personal data.

4. Processors and hosting

Contact form submissions are processed by Web3Forms and sent to our business inbox. This website is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may process technical logs required for hosting and security.

5. Your rights

Under UK GDPR, you may have rights including access, correction, and erasure of your personal data (subject to legal limits). To make a request, contact hello@logiccore.cloud.
